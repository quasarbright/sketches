Mirror

Your camera, cropped to fill the window and flipped left-to-right so it behaves the way a mirror does. Nothing is done to the picture.

the red lamp

A tally light is the lamp on a broadcast camera that tells the person in front of it the camera is live. The one in the corner here works the same way: it's lit while frames are actually arriving from your camera, and it goes dark the moment they stop — camera unplugged, another app grabbing it, a browser tab throttled in the background. The numbers under it are the real ones the camera reports: the size of the picture it's sending, how many frames per second are arriving, and the device's own name for itself.

where the video goes

Nowhere. The stream is read straight into this page and drawn on screen. There's no server involved, no recording, and snapshots are saved by your browser to your own downloads.
