Shunting Trainyard

Draft. A shunting puzzle: cars can't move by themselves, so the only way to rearrange a train is to drive a locomotive back and forth, throwing switches and coupling and uncoupling cars as you go.

The move count goes up by one each time the loco starts driving in a direction. Par is the fewest moves possible, found by searching every position the yard can reach.
